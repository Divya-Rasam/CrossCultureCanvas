About Cross Culture Canvas

A platform built to amplify underground artists and connect them with venues & event organizers.

What We Do

We bridge the gap between underground talent and real-world opportunities. Whether you're a rapper, graffiti artist, DJ, or skateboarder, we give you the tools to showcase your work, connect with venues, and get booked.

For venue owners and event organizers, we make it easy to discover, book, and promote authentic underground talent.

Who It's For

Artists

Create your profile, upload media, and get booked for gigs, exhibitions, and showcases.

Venue Owners

List your space, manage bookings, and discover talented artists ready to perform.

Event Organizers

Browse artists and venues, create events, and manage everything in one place.

How It Works

1
Create Your Profile

Artists upload media. Venues list their space. Organizers plan events.

2
Connect & Book

Artists apply to venues. Venue owners approve bookings. Everyone gets notified.

3
Host & Perform

Events go live. Artists perform. The underground scene grows.
